# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 5, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Directus Mobile** (the “App”), published by [Kurbo Web Agency](https://kurbo.dev) (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the App or visit [directusmobile.app](https://directusmobile.app).

Directus Mobile is a companion app for **your own Directus backend**. Your project content stays on the Directus servers you configure. We do not operate a shared Directus Mobile cloud that stores your CMS content.

## Information we process

### Account and connection details (on your device)

To connect to your Directus instance(s), the App stores connection and session information locally on your device, such as:

- Directus server URL(s)
- Authentication credentials or tokens needed to stay signed in
- App preferences and settings (for example push and widget configuration)

This information is stored on your device (and, where needed for widgets, in on-device shared storage such as an App Group). It is used to talk to **your** Directus server(s), not to send your CMS content to us.

### Your Directus content

Content you browse, create, edit, or delete in the App is requested from and saved to the Directus instance you choose. Access is controlled by that server’s permissions. We do not receive a copy of your project data as part of normal App use.

### Push notifications

If you enable push notifications:

- Your device’s push token may be registered with **your** Directus project (via collections/flows you or an admin install)
- Notification delivery may go through Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and/or Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), and through a push sender you or your project configures

We process push-related technical data only as needed to deliver notifications you opted into. What is stored on your Directus server is governed by that project’s own policies.

### Home screen widgets

Widgets read configuration and display data using on-device shared storage and requests to your Directus instance. Widget setup does not move your project data to a shared Directus Mobile backend.

### Website analytics

On [directusmobile.app](https://directusmobile.app) in production, we may use privacy-conscious analytics (currently via Kurbo analytics) to understand aggregate traffic and improve the site. This is separate from App CMS data.

### App stores and platforms

Apple, Google, and other platform providers may collect device and usage information under their own privacy policies when you download or use the App.

## How we use information

We use information only to:

- Provide and improve Directus Mobile and the marketing website
- Deliver optional features you enable (such as push notifications)
- Maintain security and diagnose technical issues
- Comply with legal obligations when required

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Data retention

- Local App data remains on your device until you sign out, clear App data, or uninstall the App
- Data on your Directus server is retained according to that project’s configuration and policies
- Website analytics may be retained in aggregate form for a limited period for reporting

## Sharing

We do not share your CMS content with third parties. Technical service providers involved in App distribution, push delivery (Apple/Google), or website hosting/analytics may process limited technical data as needed to provide those services.

## Security

We take reasonable steps to protect credentials stored on device and communications with your servers. You are responsible for securing your Directus instance, network, and account credentials.

## Children’s privacy

Directus Mobile is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices

- Disconnect servers or clear local App data / uninstall to remove on-device connection data
- Disable push notifications in system or App settings
- Manage what your Directus project stores (including push device records) through Directus admin tools and role permissions

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App or website after changes means you accept the updated policy.
